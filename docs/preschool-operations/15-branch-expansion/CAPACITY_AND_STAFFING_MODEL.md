# Branch Capacity & Staffing Model

**Status:** Draft framework

## Principle

Capacity is a controlled operating decision, not simply the number of children that physically fit inside a room.

## Capacity inputs

- approved programme mix: Nursery / LKG / UKG and other services
- usable learning space
- room configuration
- age and developmental needs
- applicable adult-to-child requirements
- Bcube quality standards
- supervision complexity
- arrival / dispersal capacity
- washroom and shared-area capacity
- staff availability and absence coverage
- timetable / shift design

## Capacity hierarchy

`Physical capacity → Regulatory capacity → Safe operational capacity → Quality capacity → Sellable capacity`

The lowest applicable limit governs.

## Staffing layers

### Branch leadership
- Branch Head / Centre Head
- Academic Coordinator where scale requires

### Academic
- Lead / class teachers
- Assistant teachers / classroom support based on programme and approved staffing plan
- specialist/activity support where adopted

### Admissions / administration
- Admissions / parent relationship role
- Administration / operations support

### Support
- housekeeping
- security / access support where required
- transport staff where applicable
- other facility support

## Staffing controls

- approved staffing plan before launch
- verified onboarding and required background checks
- induction before unsupervised responsibility
- substitute / absence coverage plan
- opening and closing coverage
- emergency role assignments
- no enrolment increase beyond approved operating capacity without revalidation

## Workforce planning metrics

- enrolled children by programme
- active children vs approved capacity
- teacher / support staffing against plan
- vacancy rate
- staff absence rate
- substitute coverage
- teacher turnover
- training completion
