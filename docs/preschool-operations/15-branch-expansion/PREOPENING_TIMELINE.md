# Branch Pre-opening Timeline

**Status:** Draft framework

This is a relative planning model. Actual durations depend on site, approvals, construction scope, hiring market and local requirements.

## T-12 to T-9 weeks — Mobilize

- confirm approved site / commercial terms
- appoint launch owner and workstream owners
- initiate design / fit-out
- begin compliance / approval work
- approve programme/capacity hypothesis
- begin leadership recruitment
- establish launch budget and reporting

## T-8 to T-6 weeks — Build & recruit

- progress fit-out and services
- recruit teachers / operations roles
- initiate procurement
- prepare technology/network plan
- activate marketing / enquiry capture
- configure admissions process

## T-5 to T-3 weeks — Operationalize

- staff onboarding / verification
- teacher induction and academic training
- receive learning materials / furniture / equipment
- technology setup
- parent counselling / campus visit process
- build opening roster
- validate safety / emergency procedures

## T-2 weeks — Integrate

- classroom setup
- full staff walkthrough
- EduOS workflow testing
- inventory verification
- opening / closing drill
- arrival / dispersal simulation
- incident / emergency tabletop exercise
- parent orientation preparation

## T-1 week — Readiness

- formal cross-functional readiness audit
- classify findings Critical / High / Medium / Low
- close critical findings
- finalize child/staff rosters
- verify parent communications
- confirm first-week lesson plans
- confirm opening-day command structure

## Opening week

- daily launch huddle
- attendance and handover reconciliation
- parent issue triage
- classroom observation
- facilities/safety inspection
- technology/support watch
- daily corrective-action review

## Post-opening

Use 30-, 60- and 90-day reviews to transition from launch governance to normal branch governance.
