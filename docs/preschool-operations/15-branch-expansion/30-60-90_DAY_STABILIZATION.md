# 30 / 60 / 90-Day Branch Stabilization

**Status:** Draft v0.1

## Purpose

Ensure a newly opened branch reaches stable, repeatable operation rather than exiting launch governance immediately after opening.

## Day 0–30 — Stabilize basics

Focus:
- child safety / handover discipline
- staff attendance and role clarity
- classroom routine consistency
- parent onboarding / communication
- admissions pipeline
- fee and record reconciliation
- facility defects
- technology access / workflow issues

Review weekly during the first month.

## Day 31–60 — Improve quality

Focus:
- classroom observation and coaching
- curriculum progress
- student observation quality
- parent satisfaction themes
- staff training gaps
- admissions conversion
- procurement / inventory consumption
- operating expense variance
- recurring incident/root-cause patterns

## Day 61–90 — Validate operating maturity

Focus:
- academic quality trend
- enrolment vs approved ramp
- retention / withdrawal signals
- teacher stability
- parent experience
- fee collection
- audit compliance
- safety corrective-action closure
- branch dashboard reliability
- local process deviations

## 90-day exit criteria

A branch may transition to normal governance when:
- no unresolved critical safety findings
- key operating roles are stable or controlled coverage exists
- attendance / arrival / dispersal controls are functioning consistently
- academic planning and classroom delivery rhythm is established
- parent issue handling is functioning
- finance and admission records reconcile
- critical systems and fallbacks work
- management KPIs are available and reviewed
- remaining improvement actions have owners and dates
