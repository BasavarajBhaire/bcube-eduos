# Site Selection & Feasibility Framework

**Status:** Draft v0.1

## Objective

Evaluate potential preschool sites consistently across demand, child experience, safety, operations and economics.

## Evaluation dimensions

| Dimension | Example considerations |
|---|---|
| Catchment | target-family density, residential growth, commute patterns |
| Accessibility | approach roads, safe arrival/dispersal, parking/drop-off feasibility |
| Visibility | discoverability and parent confidence |
| Space | usable learning area, circulation, storage, administration and support areas |
| Child safety | controlled access, safe movement, emergency egress feasibility, hazards |
| Environment | daylight, ventilation, noise, surrounding activities |
| Utilities | power, water, sanitation, connectivity and backup needs |
| Adaptability | fit-out complexity and ability to meet Bcube space standards |
| Commercial | rent, deposit, escalation, tenure, fit-out obligations, exit risk |
| Regulatory | ability to satisfy applicable approvals and local requirements |
| Competition | nearby preschool options, positioning and unmet needs |
| Economics | capacity, achievable fee assumptions, enrolment ramp and break-even sensitivity |

## Site scorecard

Use a weighted scorecard, but maintain **hard-stop criteria** for unacceptable safety, legal/regulatory or operational constraints. A high commercial score cannot compensate for a hard-stop failure.

## Feasibility scenarios

Model at minimum:
- conservative enrolment
- base enrolment
- target enrolment
- delayed-ramp scenario

Do not treat maximum physical seating as sellable capacity; operational capacity must reflect programme mix, staffing, safety, circulation and quality standards.

## Required evidence before approval

- site photographs / plans
- measured usable areas
- commercial term sheet
- initial fit-out estimate
- catchment / competition assessment
- capacity hypothesis
- regulatory feasibility review
- documented risks and assumptions
- approval record
