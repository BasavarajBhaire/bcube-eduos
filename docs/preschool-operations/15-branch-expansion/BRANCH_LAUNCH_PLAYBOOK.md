# Bcube Future Preschool — Branch Launch Playbook

**Status:** Draft v0.1  
**Owner:** Central Operations / Expansion Lead

## Purpose

Provide a repeatable stage-gated process for taking a Bcube Future Preschool location from opportunity identification to stable operation.

## Launch lifecycle

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Strategy --> Market[Market / Catchment]
  Market --> Site[Site Evaluation]
  Site --> Feasibility[Business Feasibility]
  Feasibility --> Approve[Investment Approval]
  Approve --> Design[Space & Systems Design]
  Design --> Build[Fit-out / Setup]
  Build --> Recruit[Recruit & Train]
  Recruit --> Admissions[Pre-opening Admissions]
  Admissions --> Readiness[Readiness Audit]
  Readiness --> Launch[Opening]
  Launch --> Stabilize[30/60/90-day Stabilization]
  Stabilize --> BAU[Business as Usual]
```

## Stage gates

### Gate 1 — Market fit
Validate target families, child population, competitive landscape, accessibility, visibility, demand assumptions and fee-positioning hypothesis.

### Gate 2 — Site feasibility
Validate usable space, access, child movement, emergency access, utilities, structural/fit-out constraints, landlord/commercial terms and regulatory feasibility. No site should be approved on rent economics alone.

### Gate 3 — Business approval
Approve capacity assumptions, launch budget, recurring cost base, staffing model, enrolment ramp and downside scenario.

### Gate 4 — Design freeze
Approve floor plan, classroom capacity, learning zones, child-safe circulation, washrooms, reception/access, storage, staff areas, technology/network locations, security/CCTV plan where lawful and appropriate, signage and brand application.

### Gate 5 — Operational readiness
Confirm facility, people, academic materials, safety controls, systems, admissions, parent communication and opening-day procedures are ready.

### Gate 6 — Launch authorization
Opening is authorized only after critical readiness findings are closed or explicitly accepted by the authorized accountable role. Child-safety critical findings cannot be waived for schedule convenience.

## Workstreams

- Site / commercial

- Facilities / safety
- Academics / curriculum

- Procurement / inventory

## Stabilization

The branch remains in launch governance through the 90-day review. The goal is not merely to open the doors; it is to reach controlled, repeatable operation with stable staffing, parent experience, academic delivery, safety controls and financial visibility.
