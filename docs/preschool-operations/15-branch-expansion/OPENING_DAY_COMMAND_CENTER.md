# Opening-Day Command Center

**Status:** Draft v0.1

## Objective

Provide enhanced coordination during the first days of a new branch while normal operating routines stabilize.

## Command roles

- Launch Lead — overall coordination / decisions
- Branch Head — branch execution
- Academic Lead — classroom delivery / teacher support
- Operations & Safety Lead — facility, arrival/dispersal and incidents
- Admissions / Parent Lead — parent questions and onboarding
- Technology Support — systems/access/connectivity
- Finance Support — authorized fee/payment exceptions

One person may hold multiple roles in a small launch, but accountability must remain explicit.

## Opening-day checkpoints

### Before arrival
- campus opening inspection complete
- staff attendance and role allocation confirmed
- child roster / authorized pickup information available
- classrooms and materials ready
- systems tested
- known parent exceptions reviewed

### Arrival window
- control congestion
- verify handover
- reconcile attendance
- rapidly escalate child-safety or identity uncertainty
- separate parent questions from the child handover line where possible

### During school
- classroom observation / support
- facilities check
- attendance reconciliation
- parent issues triaged
- technology failures routed without disrupting safe operation

### Dispersal
- authorized handover control
- no shortcut because of queue pressure
- reconcile children remaining on campus
- record exceptions/incidents

### End of day
Hold a short launch review:
- safety / incidents
- attendance / handover exceptions
- parent issues
- academic delivery
- staff gaps
- facilities / inventory
- technology
- actions for next day

## Issue severity

**Critical:** immediate child safety, safeguarding, serious facility/emergency or inability to operate safely.  
**High:** major operational failure with significant impact but safe workaround available.  
**Medium:** material issue that does not prevent safe operation.  
**Low:** improvement / cosmetic / optimization.

Critical issues take priority over schedule, parent convenience and launch optics.
