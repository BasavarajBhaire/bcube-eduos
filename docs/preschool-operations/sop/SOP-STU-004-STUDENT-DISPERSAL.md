# SOP-STU-004 — Student Dispersal & Authorized Handover

## 1. Purpose

Ensure every child leaves the preschool only through a controlled, verifiable handover process.

## 2. Scope

Applies to normal pickup, early pickup and unexpected pickup changes at every Bcube Future Preschool branch.

## 3. Roles

- **Branch Head:** accountable for implementation and exceptions.
- **Teacher/designated staff:** maintains supervision and completes approved handover.
- **Front office/admin:** supports identity/authorization verification and records exceptions.
- **Parent/guardian:** maintains current authorized-pickup information and communicates changes through approved channels.

## 4. Preconditions

- Current student attendance available.
- Current authorized-pickup information available through the approved source.
- Designated dispersal area and responsible staff established.

## 5. Procedure

1. Prepare children for dispersal while maintaining supervision.
2. Receive the pickup request at the approved handover point.
3. Verify that the person is authorized using the approved branch mechanism.
4. If verification succeeds, call/prepare the child without weakening supervision of remaining children.
5. Complete the handover and required departure record.
6. Reconcile children remaining after the main dispersal window.
7. Escalate unresolved late pickup or authorization exceptions to the Branch Head.

## 6. Unexpected / unauthorized pickup

If the person cannot be verified as authorized:

1. **Do not release the child.**
2. Keep the child supervised in the approved safe area.
3. Contact the authorized parent/guardian through the approved contact information/process.
4. Apply any additional verification required by current policy.
5. Branch Head or designated authority handles the exception.
6. Record the exception when required.

Staff must not bypass this control because the person is familiar, persuasive, in a hurry or claims a verbal arrangement that cannot be verified.

## 7. Early pickup

Early pickup follows the same authorization requirement and must update the attendance/departure record so the branch can accurately account for children on site.

## 8. Controls

- Active supervision throughout dispersal
- Approved authorization source
- No informal override of failed verification
- Departure / exception record where required
- End-of-dispersal reconciliation

## 9. Evidence

- Attendance record
- Authorized pickup record
- Departure/early-pickup record as applicable
- Exception/incident record where applicable

## 10. Escalation

Any safety concern, custody dispute, threatening behavior, suspected safeguarding issue or inability to establish safe handover must be escalated immediately under the relevant safety/emergency process. Applicable legal/custody requirements must be handled according to verified documentation and professional/legal guidance.

## 11. KPIs

- Unauthorized release incidents: target **zero**
- Dispersal reconciliation completion
- Authorization exceptions by category
- Late pickup trend
