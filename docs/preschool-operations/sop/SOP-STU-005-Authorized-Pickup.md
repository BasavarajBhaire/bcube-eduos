# SOP-STU-005 — Authorized Pickup

**Status:** Draft v0.1 — safety-sensitive; validate locally before activation.  
**Owner:** Branch Head / Dispersal Lead

## Non-negotiable outcome
A child leaves school only through a controlled handover to an appropriately authorized adult under the approved school process.

## Procedure
1. Maintain the authoritative authorized-pickup information using the approved student record process.
2. At dispersal, identify the collecting adult using the branch's approved verification method.
3. Match the adult to current authorization before releasing the child.
4. Record the handover/departure where required by the operating model.
5. For a temporary or changed pickup arrangement, require the approved parent/guardian change-verification process; do not rely solely on an informal verbal message delivered by an unknown person.
6. If identity or authorization cannot be established, keep the child supervised and escalate to the Branch Head/designated safeguarding lead.
7. Emergency exceptions must be documented after appropriate verification and approval.

## Prohibited shortcuts
- Release based only on familiarity without required verification.
- Ask the child to decide whether an unknown adult may collect them.
- Accept an unverified third-party message as sufficient authorization.
- Allow pressure, delay or inconvenience to override the safety control.

## Evidence
Current authorization record; dispersal/handover record; temporary-change evidence; exception/incident record.

## Metrics
Authorization exceptions; attempted unauthorized pickups; handover incidents; unresolved pickup changes.
