# SOP-FIN-001 — Fee Collection

**Status:** Draft v0.1 — commercial/tax treatment requires validation before activation.  
**Owner:** Finance / Authorized Branch Administrator

## Purpose
Ensure student fees are assessed, collected, receipted, reconciled and reported consistently with approved fee terms and financial controls.

## Procedure
1. Use only the approved fee schedule and authorized concessions/discounts.
2. Generate or record the student's payable amount against the authoritative student/account record.
3. Accept payment only through approved channels.
4. Issue an appropriate receipt/acknowledgement and record payment reference, amount, date and method.
5. Reconcile payment records against bank/gateway/cash records as applicable.
6. Investigate unmatched, duplicate, failed or reversed transactions.
7. Maintain an outstanding-fee view and follow the approved reminder/escalation process.
8. Refunds, waivers and material adjustments require documented authorization according to the Delegation of Authority.
9. Complete periodic finance reconciliation and exception review.

## Controls
- The person approving a discretionary financial adjustment should not self-approve their own request.
- No unofficial fee promises or unrecorded discounts.
- Cash handling, if permitted, requires additional custody, receipt and reconciliation controls.
- Student/parent financial information is access-restricted.

## Evidence
Approved fee schedule; invoice/demand where applicable; payment reference; receipt; reconciliation; concession/refund approval; outstanding balance.

## KPIs
Collection rate; overdue balance; reconciliation exceptions; failed/reversed payments; unauthorized adjustment exceptions.
