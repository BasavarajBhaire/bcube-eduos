# SOP-SAF-006 — Safety / Accident / Incident Management

**Status:** Draft v0.1 — requires jurisdiction-specific safeguarding, medical and legal validation.  
**Owner:** Branch Head / Designated Safety or Safeguarding Lead

## Priority order
**Protect people → obtain appropriate emergency/medical assistance → escalate → notify as required → preserve facts → document → investigate → correct.**

## Procedure
1. Remove or control immediate danger without creating additional risk.
2. Provide appropriate first response within staff training and obtain emergency/medical assistance when required.
3. Ensure affected children remain supervised and supported.
4. Escalate immediately to the responsible school leader and safeguarding lead when applicable.
5. Notify parents/guardians and authorities/professionals when required by approved policy and applicable rules.
6. Record factual observations, time, location, persons involved, immediate actions and witnesses. Separate facts from assumptions.
7. Preserve relevant records/evidence with appropriate privacy controls.
8. Determine whether the event requires formal investigation or safeguarding review.
9. Identify contributing causes and corrective/preventive actions.
10. Assign action owners and due dates; verify effectiveness before closure.

## Severity model
- **Critical:** immediate serious threat to life/safety, missing child, suspected serious safeguarding event or other emergency requiring immediate escalation.
- **High:** material injury/safety event requiring urgent leadership response.
- **Moderate:** incident requiring documented follow-up/correction but no continuing serious danger.
- **Low / near miss:** no injury or limited consequence, but useful for prevention and trend analysis.

Exact thresholds and notification requirements must be defined in approved local policy.

## Controls
- Never delay emergency action to complete documentation.
- Do not speculate or assign blame in the initial incident record.
- Restrict sensitive incident information to appropriate roles.
- Safeguarding concerns must follow the safeguarding pathway, not only the general incident workflow.

## Metrics
Incident frequency and severity; near misses; time to escalation; overdue corrective actions; recurrence rate.
