# SOP-ACA-009 — Parent Progress Reporting

**Status:** Draft v0.1

## Purpose
Give parents a clear, constructive and evidence-based understanding of their child's preschool progress and next learning steps.

## Report structure
- child strengths / interests
- progress by agreed learning domains
- specific evidence/examples
- areas currently developing
- next classroom priorities
- practical home connection where useful
- attendance/context notes when materially relevant

## Procedure
1. Review current observation and assessment evidence.
2. Validate unusual or significant judgments with the academic coordinator.
3. Use plain parent-friendly language.
4. Describe what the child does; avoid labels such as “weak”, “poor”, “slow” or unsupported diagnostic terms.
5. Balance strengths and next steps.
6. Provide realistic home suggestions that do not shift the school's teaching responsibility to parents.
7. Discuss significant concerns privately and respectfully.
8. Record agreed follow-up actions from PTM/progress discussions.

## Controls
- Only discuss the relevant child's information with authorized parent/guardian contacts.
- Do not compare the child with named classmates.
- Do not promise outcomes or diagnose conditions.
- Sensitive discussions should not occur in public/shared spaces.
