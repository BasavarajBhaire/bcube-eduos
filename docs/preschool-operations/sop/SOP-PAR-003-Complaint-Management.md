# SOP-PAR-003 — Parent Complaint & Concern Management

**Status:** Draft v0.1  
**Owner:** Branch Head

## Purpose
Resolve parent concerns fairly, promptly and consistently while using recurring issues as improvement signals.

## Classification
- **Service:** communication, administration, schedule or facility experience.
- **Academic:** teaching, learning, progress or classroom practice.
- **Finance:** fee, receipt, refund or approved commercial term.
- **Safety / safeguarding:** any concern involving potential child safety or protection risk.
- **Privacy / data:** inappropriate access, disclosure or data handling.

## Flow
```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Receive --> Record --> Classify --> Own[Assign owner]
  Own --> Investigate --> Respond --> Resolve
  Resolve --> Close[Confirm & close]
  Close --> Trend[Trend / improve]
```

## Procedure
1. Listen and capture the concern without arguing or prematurely assigning blame.
2. Record facts, requested outcome and relevant dates/evidence.
3. Classify severity and domain.
4. Safety/safeguarding concerns bypass normal service queues and use the applicable escalation procedure immediately.
5. Assign one accountable owner and communicate the next step to the parent.
6. Investigate using relevant records and responsible staff.
7. Provide a factual response, corrective action where appropriate, and expected closure timing.
8. Record resolution and whether the parent was informed.
9. Trend recurring concerns and open corrective actions for systemic issues.

## Controls
- Protect confidentiality and limit access to those who need it.
- Do not alter records to make an issue appear resolved.
- Avoid retaliation or adverse treatment because a parent raised a concern.
- Material incidents should link to incident/corrective-action records rather than being closed only as a complaint.

## KPIs
Acknowledgement time; resolution time; reopen rate; complaint rate; recurring themes; corrective-action closure.
