# SOP-ACA-007 — Student Observation

**Status:** Draft v0.1  
**Owner:** Class Teacher / Academic Coordinator

## Purpose
Capture useful evidence of children's learning and development through normal preschool experiences rather than relying only on formal tests or completed pages.

## Observation model

`Learning objective → Child opportunity → Observable behaviour/work → Teacher note → Interpretation → Teaching response`

## Procedure
1. Identify the learning outcome or behaviour to observe.
2. Observe during normal classroom, play, communication, creative or routine contexts where possible.
3. Record concise factual evidence before interpretation.
4. Distinguish a one-time observation from a consistent pattern.
5. Use multiple opportunities before making significant progress judgments.
6. Record support provided if it materially affected the child's response.
7. Translate the observation into a teaching response: continue, reinforce, provide another context, enrich or seek academic leadership guidance.
8. Escalate wellbeing/safeguarding concerns through the relevant process rather than treating them as ordinary academic notes.

## Suggested progress language
Use a small controlled scale such as:
- **Emerging** — beginning with significant modelling/support
- **Developing** — demonstrates with some prompting or in familiar contexts
- **Secure** — demonstrates independently and consistently in expected contexts
- **Extending** — applies or explains the learning in a broader/new context

The scale should describe evidence, not label the child.

## Controls
- Avoid diagnosing developmental/medical conditions from classroom observation.
- Avoid comparing children publicly.
- Keep sensitive child observations access-controlled.
- Significant concerns require appropriate qualified/professional pathways.

## KPIs
- observation coverage across children/outcomes
- overdue progress evidence
- teaching adaptations linked to observations
