## Rating guidance

- **1 — Needs immediate development:** expected practice is substantially absent or ineffective
- **2 — Developing:** practice is present inconsistently and needs coaching
- **3 — Effective:** expected practice is consistently evident
- **4 — Strong practice:** highly effective, responsive practice worth sharing
- **N/O — Not observed:** insufficient evidence in this observation
