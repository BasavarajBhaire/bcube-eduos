# Academic KPI Catalog

**Status:** Draft — targets should be baselined before becoming performance commitments.

| KPI | Definition | Frequency | Primary owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual plan readiness | Approved academic plan before programme start | Annual | Academic Head |
| Weekly plan readiness | Classes with reviewed plan before delivery window | Weekly | Academic Coordinator |
| Curriculum progress | Approved planned outcomes delivered vs planned | Weekly/Monthly | Academic Coordinator |
| Observation coverage | Children with sufficient current observation evidence | Monthly | Class Teacher |
| Progress review completion | Scheduled progress reviews completed | Term/period | Academic Coordinator |
| Classroom observation completion | Planned teacher observations completed | Monthly/Term | Academic Coordinator |
| Coaching action closure | Teacher development actions completed by due date | Monthly | Academic Coordinator |
| Repeat teaching finding | Repeated observation issue after agreed coaching | Monthly/Term | Academic Head |
| Material readiness | Planned activities with required materials ready | Weekly | Teacher / Operations |
| Content issue rate | Validated curriculum/content issues raised from delivery | Monthly | Academic Head |
| Content feedback closure | Validated curriculum issues resolved/triaged | Monthly | Curriculum Owner |
| Parent progress reporting | Scheduled reports/PTMs completed | Term/period | Branch Head |

## Metric rules

- Define numerator, denominator and exclusions before dashboard implementation.
- Do not reward curriculum completion at the expense of meaningful learning.
- Do not use child progress measures to rank teachers without context and robust methodology.
- Safety/safeguarding indicators remain separate non-negotiable controls.
