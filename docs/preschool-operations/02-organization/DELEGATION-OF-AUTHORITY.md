# Delegation of Authority (DoA) Framework

**Status:** Draft v0.1 — thresholds and named roles must be approved before activation.

## Purpose
Define which decisions may be made at branch level, which require central approval and which must always be escalated.

## Principles
- Authority follows role, not personal familiarity.
- No person may approve a transaction/action solely for their own benefit where separation is reasonably possible.
- Safety and safeguarding escalation cannot be suppressed by commercial or hierarchy concerns.
- Financial limits must be explicit numeric thresholds in the approved DoA, not informal custom.
- Exceptions require documented rationale and an authorized approver.

## Decision classes

| Decision | Branch execution | Branch approval | Central / specialist approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routine admission within approved criteria | Admissions | Branch Head | — |
| Admission exception | Prepare | Recommend | Authorized academic/management role |
| Standard fee collection | Admin/Finance | Per process | — |
| Discount/concession | Prepare | Within approved threshold only | Above threshold |
| Refund/financial adjustment | Prepare | Within approved threshold only | Above threshold / Finance |
| Routine procurement | Request | Within approved threshold | Above threshold |
| New vendor | Evaluate | Recommend | Per procurement policy |
| Staff leave | Employee/manager | Defined manager | HR for exceptions |
| Recruitment selection | Interview panel | Recommend | Authorized hiring approver |
| System access | Request | Role owner | System/data owner as required |
| Safety/safeguarding event | Immediate protective action | Immediate escalation | Designated safeguarding/specialist authority |
| Public/media statement | No unauthorized release | — | Authorized central role |

## Approval matrix fields to finalize
For every controlled decision define: decision ID, category, amount/risk threshold, requester, reviewer, approver, evidence, exception approver and review frequency.
