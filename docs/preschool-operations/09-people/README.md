# People & Teacher Operations

**Status:** Draft v0.1

## Objective

Recruit, prepare, support and retain people capable of delivering Bcube standards consistently and safely.

## Employee lifecycle

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    Plan[Workforce plan] --> Recruit
    Recruit --> Select
    Select --> Verify[Required verification]
    Verify --> Onboard
    Onboard --> Train
    Train --> Observe[Coaching / observation]
    Observe --> Perform[Performance review]
    Perform --> Develop[Development]
    Develop --> Retain[Retention / progression]
    Retain --> Exit
```

## Teacher readiness framework

A teacher should be prepared across:

- child safety and safeguarding
- Bcube educational philosophy
- curriculum and learning outcomes
- classroom routines and supervision
- lesson/activity preparation
- observation and assessment
- parent communication
- incident and escalation procedures
- data/privacy responsibilities
- approved EduOS tools where applicable

## Onboarding minimums

Before independent responsibility, complete applicable identity/employment records, required background/eligibility verification, role orientation, safeguarding/safety training, emergency procedures, classroom expectations and access provisioning.

## Classroom quality cycle

`Plan → Deliver → Observe → Coach → Improve → Re-observe`

Observation is intended to improve teaching quality and consistency, not merely produce a score.

## Performance dimensions

- Child safety and supervision
- Learning delivery quality
- Classroom environment
- Planning/readiness
- Observation and record quality
- Parent communication
- Team collaboration
- Reliability / attendance
- Training and improvement
- Compliance with approved procedures

## People KPIs

- Staffing against approved requirement
- Time to fill critical roles
- Onboarding completion
- Mandatory training completion
- Staff attendance
- Classroom observation completion / quality trends
- Development-action closure
- Retention / turnover

Employment procedures and background-verification requirements must be aligned to applicable law and qualified HR/legal guidance before adoption.
